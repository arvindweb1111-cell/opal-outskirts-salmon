About Karissa

Born & Raised in Salmon — Finally Home

Karissa Barney grew up in Salmon, trained at Austin Kade Academy, and honed her skills at Ulta Beauty before coming back home to open Opal Outskirts Salon in January 2025.

Opal Outskirts is more than a salon — it's Karissa's love letter to Salmon. She brings big-city training and genuine small-town heart to every single client who sits in her chair.

With a perfect 5-star rating from her very first clients, Karissa is already making her mark on Salmon's beauty scene. Follow her on Instagram to see her latest work!
